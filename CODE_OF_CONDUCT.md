# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in HexNest a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

**Expected:** Respectful language, welcoming of different viewpoints, graceful acceptance of feedback, focus on what is best for the community.

**Not acceptable:** Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, sexualized language, or other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported via GitHub issues (mark as `[CoC]`). Maintainers will review within 7 days.
